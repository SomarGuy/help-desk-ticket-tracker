How these numbers are worked out

Time to resolve is measured from when a ticket was created to when it was resolved or closed. "Within SLA" compares that moment with the due time set by the priority (sample policy: Critical 4h, High 8h, Medium 24h, Low 72h). The tickets are synthetic, so none of this says anything about real performance.
